# Exercise F: meeting notes

For [task F. From notes to a decision paper](../../tasks/f-notes-to-decision-paper.md).

`meeting-notes.md` holds made-up notes from a meeting about the digitisation queue. The notes are messy on purpose: there are rough numbers, things nobody has checked, and the people at the meeting disagree. A decision is needed, and the section lead wants a decision paper for the leadership group.

Ask the agent for a decision paper of at most one page. Then check:

- Did it make up numbers that aren't in the notes?
- Did it notice what is still unconfirmed (the framework agreement price, the operations budget)?
- Does the recommendation follow from the options?
